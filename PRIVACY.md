# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Cache Cleaner does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, or internet permission.

Cache size checks and cache deletion happen locally on the device and are limited to Cache Cleaner's own app-specific cache directories. The app does not access or clear other apps' data. It does not store or transmit the displayed cache size, usage information, or diagnostics.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the publisher through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sajad3731/android-cache-cleaner/issues).
