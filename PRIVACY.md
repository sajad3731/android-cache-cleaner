# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Cache Cleaner does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or internet permission, and includes no third-party service.

## Permissions and what they are used for

- **Usage access** (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`): lets Android report each installed app's cache size to Cache Cleaner. The app does not read app usage history.
- **Installed apps list** (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`): used only to show each app's name, icon, and cache size in the scan results, and to tell which folders belong to apps that are no longer installed.
- **Accessibility service**: used only to open the Storage page of each app you chose to clean in Android Settings and press Clear cache. It acts only on Settings screens and only while a cleaning you started is running. It does not read, keep, or send any other screen content.
- **All files access** (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`; storage permission on Android 10 and older): used only by Junk files, to find leftover folders, app installers, thumbnail caches, empty folders, and old large downloads on the phone's shared storage, and to delete the ones you choose. File names are only shown on screen. App-private folders (`Android/data`, `Android/obb`) are never accessed.
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): used only for the cleaning reminder, if you turn it on.
- **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): keeps the cleaning reminder after the phone restarts.

All scanning and cleaning happens on the device. Your settings (size limit, apps to skip, reminder) and a history of cleanings (date, freed space, number of apps) are kept on the device only, and you can clear the history in the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the publisher through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sajad3731/android-cache-cleaner/issues).
